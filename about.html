In remembrance

Dr. Jambulingam Subramani

From humble beginnings, Dr. Jambulingam Subramani built a life devoted to mathematics, research and education.

His professional journey began as a research assistant in the Department of Statistics at the University of Madras. He went on to work as a Quality Manager at RTSSL, gaining practical experience in industrial quality management. Returning to academia, he served as Head of the Faculty of Mathematics at Kalasalingam before joining Pondicherry University, where he served as an Associate Professor and Head of the Department of Statistics.

A passionate researcher and educator, his contributions spanned sampling theory, statistical estimation, experimental design, industrial quality control and recreational mathematics. His dedication earned him recognition through the 2014 Pearson Teaching Awards and the ASDF Best Academic Researcher Award.

Though he is no longer with us, his ideas and contributions live on. This website is a small tribute to his journey, his unwavering passion for research and his love of mathematics. It seeks to celebrate his work by making his ideas accessible to everyone with a curiosity to learn.
