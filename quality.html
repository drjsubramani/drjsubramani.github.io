04 / Quality control

The price of perfection.

Every manufactured item varies a little. But when does ordinary variation become evidence that a machine needs attention?

Introduce a shift in the production process and watch the control chart respond.

Before

Inspecting every shipment consumes time even when production has a reliable quality history. Skip-lot inspection already existed.

His idea · with S. Balamurali, 2012

They designed parameters for SkSP-2 using double sampling as the reference inspection, balancing the risk of rejecting good lots against accepting bad ones. Their 2018 work optimized inspection effort for normally distributed measurements.

Watch inspection rules change

Illustrative thresholds: first 5 items, accept 0 defects, reject 2 or more; otherwise inspect 5 more and accept at most 1 total defect. After 3 accepted lots inspect a random half; rejection restores normal inspection. Not optimized published parameters.

Related research: monitoring process drift

Illustrative fixed control limits, not specification limits. A signal calls for investigation, not automatic rejection.
